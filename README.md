Railway transportation is one of the most widely used modes of travel in India, serving millions of passengers every day. However, train delays remain a persistent challenge due to factors such as adverse weather conditions, heavy rail traffic, festivals, operational constraints, and seasonal passenger demand. These delays often disrupt travel plans and make it difficult for passengers to manage connecting transportation and schedules.
This project, "Synapse", aims to develop an intelligent predictive system that estimates train delays before a journey begins. By analyzing historical railway data together with contextual information such as weather forecasts, weekends, public holidays, religious festivals, and previous delay patterns, the system provides accurate delay predictions for selected stations along a train's route. In addition to predicting expected delays, the system also provides confidence scores and highlights the key factors influencing each prediction, improving transparency and user trust.
Initially focusing on a small number of selected trains, the Synapse demonstrates the practical application of machine learning in railway transportation while maintaining high prediction accuracy. The model is also designed for continuous improvement through periodic retraining with newly collected data, enabling it to adapt to changing railway conditions over time. By helping passengers make informed travel decisions, this project showcases how artificial intelligence can enhance the efficiency, reliability, and convenience of modern railway transportation.
